## NOTES:

1. **All measurements taken from technical drawings** - "All measurements TBC on site and could have an effect on layout and quotation" (as noted on drawings)

2. **Material quality assumptions:**
   - Standard 16mm white melamine/supawood for carcasses
   - Wood grain melamine for decorative doors (as shown in Drawing 5)
   - 38mm postformed laminate benchtops
   - Soft-close hardware throughout

3. **Pricing based on 2026 South African market rates:**
   - Prices may vary based on supplier and location
   - Durbanville area pricing considered

4. **Exclusions:**
   - Appliances (stove, microwave, etc.)
   - Plumbing modifications
   - Electrical work
   - Wall preparation/painting
   - Removal of existing cupboards
   - Transportation costs

5. **Installation timeline:** Estimated 2-3 weeks from material order to completion

6. **Warranty:** Standard 12-month warranty on workmanship and materials
