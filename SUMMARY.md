## What's Included:

### 1. Sheet Materials: **R 36,510**
- 31 sheets of 16mm white melamine (2440x1220mm)
- 12 sheets of 6mm backing board
- 2 benchtop slabs (38mm laminated, 3050x600mm)
- 1 sheet of wood grain melamine for decorative doors

### 2. Hardware: **R 14,185**
- 85 pairs of soft-close hinges
- 24 pairs of soft-close drawer runners
- 2 chrome hanging rails
- 80 modern handles (brushed nickel)
- 200 shelf pins

### 3. Consumables: **R 6,740**
- 430 meters of edge banding
- Wood glue, contact adhesive
- Confirmat screws

### 4. Wastage Contingency (10%): **R 5,744**
